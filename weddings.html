FAQ

What couples ask first

Can we rent the entire hotel for our wedding?

Yes. Most weddings take over our Paradiso Wing — nine en-suite rooms on the beachfront. Larger groups buy out the entire hotel: all sixteen designer suites, so the gates close and no other guests share the property. Either way, it is one event at a time.

How much does a wedding at Villa Azure cost?

Private events start at $4,000, in addition to lodging. Weekend weddings require at least the 9-room Paradiso Wing with a 3-night minimum stay. Your final quote depends on the date, group size and format — check your date and we send real numbers, not ranges.

How many guests can we invite?

Up to 150 seated for the celebration, with up to 36 overnight guests across the sixteen suites. The lawn works just as well for an intimate wedding of twenty as for the full hundred and fifty. Your guest list can go beyond the overnight group — tell us your numbers and we confirm what works for your format.

Do you host destination weddings?

Most of our couples plan from abroad. The hotel is 15 minutes from San Juan’s international airport (SJU), your families stay onsite in the sixteen suites, and our planners and preferred vendors are already on the ground — a destination wedding in Puerto Rico you can plan without a single site visit.

How late can the music play?

Until midnight on Friday and Saturday, and until 10 PM on other nights. Ocean Park is a beachfront residential neighborhood, and keeping it gracious with our neighbors is part of what keeps this venue possible.

Who handles catering, flowers and music?

You work from our curated list of preferred vendors — caterers, event design, rentals and music teams who already know the property, including The Lounge, AKUA Events Design Group, Chef Chaymol and Music Factory. We also keep a list of trusted planners who can walk with you from the first call to the last dance.
