Good questions

Everything couples & planners ask us first

Straight answers about privacy, pricing and how an event at Villa Azure actually works.

Can we rent the entire hotel for a private event in San Juan?

Yes. Most events take over our Paradiso Wing — 9 en-suite rooms on the actual beachfront, with the sand steps from your door. Larger groups can buy out the entire hotel: all 16 en-suite designer suites, so the gates close and no other guests share the property. Either way, it is one event at a time.

How much does a private event at Villa Azure cost?

Private events start at $4,000, in addition to lodging. Weekend celebrations (Friday–Sunday) require a buyout of at least our 9-room beachfront Paradiso Wing with a 3-night minimum stay — and a full buyout of all 16 suites, when available, is always a possibility. Your final quote depends on the date, group size and format — check your date and we will send real numbers, not ranges.

How many guests can stay overnight?

Up to 36 overnight guests across 16 en-suite designer suites. For the celebration itself, your guest list can go beyond the overnight group — tell us your numbers and we will confirm what works for your format.

How late can the music play?

Until midnight on Friday and Saturday, and until 10 PM on other nights. Ocean Park is a beachfront residential neighborhood, and keeping it gracious with our neighbors is part of what keeps this venue possible.

Who handles catering, rentals and music?

You will work from our curated list of preferred vendors — caterers, event design, rentals and music teams who already know every corner of the property, including The Lounge, AKUA Events Design Group, Chef Chaymol and Music Factory. An event planner is ideal, and we also have a curated list of trusted planners who can assist you from the first call to the last dance.

Where do extra guests stay if the suites are full?

We arrange overflow lodging for you — at nearby Condado hotels just minutes away, and through our access to nearby vacation properties that serve beautifully as well. Your whole guest list sleeps comfortably and the celebration stays at Villa Azure.

Where exactly is the venue?

At 1 Guerrero Noble, San Juan, PR 00907 — in Ocean Park, directly on one of the city's most beautiful beaches, minutes from Condado and from San Juan international airport (SJU). Airport transfers are easily arranged through our concierge.

What happens if it rains?

Every event carries a rain plan coordinated with your planner, using the property's covered and indoor spaces — so the day moves forward beautifully either way.
