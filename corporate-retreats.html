FAQ

What teams ask first

Can we buy out the whole hotel for our team?

Yes — that is the standard way a retreat runs here. The full buyout is all sixteen designer suites, with the property closed to the public for the duration. Smaller groups often take the nine-room Paradiso Wing on the beachfront instead. Either way, it is one group at a time.

What does a corporate retreat at Villa Azure cost?

Private events start at $4,000, and that covers a full day on the property. Monday to Thursday a day event does not require lodging at all, so a local team can meet here without booking rooms. Friday to Sunday dates require at least the nine-room Paradiso Wing with a three-night minimum stay. Your quote depends on the dates, the headcount and the format — send us those and we reply with real numbers, not ranges.

How many people can we bring?

Up to 150 can be seated outdoors on the lawns, in whatever setup suits the session, and about 50 indoors. Up to 36 sleep onsite across the sixteen suites. If your team is larger than the bed count, we arrange overflow lodging at Condado hotels minutes away and the working days stay here.

Is there a meeting room — and what about AV?

There is no dedicated conference room. The living, dining and breakfast areas open into one meeting space seating about 50, and the furniture moves easily to suit the format; a second, smaller room takes a group of ten. There is no AV on the property, so screens, sound and production are rented through preferred vendors who already know every corner of it. The property has high-speed Wi-Fi. Breakouts are set up across five areas — indoors, by the pool, the beach area, the front lawn and the side lawn.

How far is the airport?

Fifteen minutes from San Juan's international airport (SJU), at 1 Calle Guerrero Noble in Ocean Park — a beachfront neighborhood of the city, not a resort strip an hour away. Teams flying in land and are at the property before lunch.

Who handles catering and production?

You work from our curated list of preferred vendors — catering, AV and production teams who have already worked the property. We also keep a list of trusted planners who can run the whole programme with you.

Your dates are either free or they aren't.

Send us the dates and the headcount. We reply with real availability and real pricing — and if the dates are open, we close the property for one group: yours.
