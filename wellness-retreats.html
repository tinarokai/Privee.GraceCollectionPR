FAQ

What a retreat leader asks first

Can we take over the whole hotel for our retreat?

Yes — and most retreats do. The full buyout is all sixteen designer suites, up to 36 people, with the property closed to the public for the duration. Smaller groups often take the nine-room Paradiso Wing on the beachfront instead. Either way, it is one retreat at a time: no other group practising on the other lawn.

What does a wellness retreat at Villa Azure cost?

The event fee starts at $4,000 and is charged once for your dates, not per day — lodging sits on top, per night. Monday to Thursday a day retreat does not require lodging at all, so a local teacher can run a day programme here without booking rooms. Friday to Sunday dates require at least the nine-room Paradiso Wing with a three-night minimum. Send us the dates and your headcount and we reply with the event fee and the lodging, which is what you need to set a spot price.

Where do sessions actually happen?

Five areas: indoors, by the pool, the beach area, the front lawn and the side lawn. Your vendors build each one to your own specification — we hold the space, you decide the setup. There is no dedicated studio: the indoor living, dining and breakfast areas convert into one space seating about fifty, and the furniture moves. Outdoors the lawns take up to 150 in whatever arrangement your practice needs.

How many people can we bring?

There are nineteen beds across the sixteen suites, and the property sleeps up to 36 people in total. So an open-enrolment retreat where everyone wants their own bed tops out around nineteen; sell shared spots, or bring a group of friends and couples, and you can reach 36. Up to 150 can be seated outdoors and about 50 indoors, so day guests can join for a workshop or a closing dinner. Beyond that we arrange overflow lodging at Condado hotels minutes away.

Can you handle retreat menus?

Yes. Menus are built with our preferred chefs around the programme you are running rather than pulled from a fixed hotel list. Tell us the shape of the days — early breakfasts, a long lunch, a quiet dinner — and what your group eats, and the kitchen is planned around it.

How far is the beach, and the airport?

Ocean Park beach is at the property line, so a sunrise session on the sand costs you no logistics at all. The airport (SJU) is fifteen minutes away, at 1 Calle Guerrero Noble in Ocean Park — a beachfront neighborhood of San Juan, not a resort strip an hour out.
